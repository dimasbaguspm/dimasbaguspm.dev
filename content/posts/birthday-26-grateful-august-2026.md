Turning 26 does not feel loud. It feels steady.

I am healthy. I am close with my parents. I get to spend real quality time with them, not just quick calls between tasks. At home, the cats are there, ready to play whenever I sit on the floor. In work, I keep going deeper into software engineering and I am still tackling AI, one problem at a time. That is a lot to be grateful for, and I do not take it for granted.

I do not feel the need to buy expensive things to mark the moment. I do not want to be in the middle of attention either. What I enjoy is simpler: a calm day, good food, people I care about nearby, and the quiet satisfaction of learning something new.

This year I spent my birthday with family at Jogja City Mall. Nothing fancy, just together. Talking, walking, laughing. Those hours matter more than any gift.
