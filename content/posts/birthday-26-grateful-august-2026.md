Turning 26 does not feel loud. It feels steady, and that is exactly what I am grateful for.

I am healthy. I am close with my parents, not just in the same house but in the same rhythm. We talk, we eat together, we make time without needing a reason. At home, Casper and Hoki are part of that rhythm too. I can sit on the floor and play with them and the day feels lighter. In work, I keep going deeper into software engineering, and I am still tackling AI, one problem at a time. Some days it clicks, some days it humbles me, and both are good teachers.

I do not feel the need to buy expensive things to mark the moment. I do not want to be in the middle of attention either. What I enjoy is simpler: a calm day, good food, people I care about nearby, and the quiet satisfaction of learning something new. If no one is looking, I am still happy. That is how I know it is real.

The day started right at midnight. At 00.00 I held a small tart cake with Casper beside me. No big party, no noise, just a quiet start to being 26. He looked at the cake like it was his, and I let him believe it for a moment.

![Midnight on my 26th birthday, holding a small tart cake with Casper](https://s3-api.dimasbaguspm.dev/personal/birthday-26-cake-casper-midnight.jpg)

Later we went out as a family. We spent time at Jogja City Mall, nothing fancy, just together. Walking, talking, laughing, letting the hours pass without rushing them. Those unplanned hours are the ones I remember most. Not what we bought, but that we were there at all, in the same place, with nowhere else to be.

![Family time at Jogja City Mall for my 26th birthday](https://s3-api.dimasbaguspm.dev/personal/birthday-26-jogja-city-mall.jpg)

We also had a meal at Oseng Ndeso. I took a selfie with Mom there, and it is one of those photos that holds more than faces. It holds that we are still making time for each other, on purpose.

![Selfie with Mom at Oseng Ndeso](https://s3-api.dimasbaguspm.dev/personal/birthday-26-mom-oseng-ndeso-selfie.jpg)

The food itself was worth remembering too. Simple, warm, the kind of meal that makes conversation last longer.

![Food at Oseng Ndeso](https://s3-api.dimasbaguspm.dev/personal/birthday-26-oseng-ndeso-food.jpg)

If I look back at 25, the lesson was to keep going. At 26, the lesson is to notice. To notice health while I have it, to notice family while they are near, to notice that playing with the cats is not a break from life but part of it, and to notice that understanding software engineering and AI is not a finish line but a daily practice.

I do not need a louder year. I need a truer one.

26 taught me this: enough is already a lot, and being grateful is how you notice it.
