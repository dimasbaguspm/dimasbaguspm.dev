**Venue:** ASTRA Honda Jombor Magelang Street
**Booked via:** Motor X app, slot 09:00, queue B010
**Verdict:** ⭐⭐ (2/5). The workshop runs, but the appointment system does not keep its word.

I booked a service slot through the Motor X app and read 09:00 as a commitment, so I arrived on time and a little early. At 09:30, half an hour past my slot, I still had not been served, and everyone else with their own booked times was in the same boat. My number B010 meant as much at 09:30 as it did at 09:00: nothing.

Weekends are packed and the bays are limited, which is understandable, but a crowd does not excuse a false promise. A timed booking only means something if the time is real, and when every slot slips, the app is either overbooking or underestimating service time. Both are the operator's failure, not the customer's. A booking is a promise with a timestamp; what I got was a suggestion with one.

Three fixes would make it honest: show the real wait time instead of a manufactured appointment, cap bookings per slot to what the bays can clear, and warn at booking if a slot will slip. The service itself may be fine once you are on the lift, but the booking sells order and delivers a queue. Book the slot if you must, but pad your morning. The clock on the app is decorative.
