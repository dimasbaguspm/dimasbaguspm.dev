**Venue:** ASTRA Honda Jombor Magelang Street
**Booked via:** Motor X app
**My slot:** 09:00, queue number B010
**Verdict:** ⭐⭐ (2/5) for the booking experience. The workshop runs, but the appointment system does not keep its word.

## What I booked

I used the Motor X app to reserve a service slot at ASTRA Honda Jombor Magelang. The app assigned me 09:00 and queue number B010. I read that as a commitment: arrive around my slot, get served around my slot.

I arrived on time, slightly early.

## What happened

At 09:30, half an hour after my booked time, I had not been served. Others with their own slots were in the same boat, all sitting past the times the app had printed for them. My number B010 carried the same weight at 09:30 that it did at 09:00: none.

## The honest part

Weekends are packed. Plenty of people want a vehicle serviced on a Saturday, and the workshop has a fixed number of bays and mechanics. That part is understandable.

## The problem

Understanding the crowd does not excuse a false promise. A timed booking only means something if the time is real. When the slot slips for everyone, one of two things is true: the app overbooks slots, or it underestimates how long each service takes. Both are the operator's failure, not the customer's.

A booking is a promise with a timestamp. What I got was a suggestion with a timestamp.

## What would fix it

- Show the real wait time instead of a manufactured appointment time.
- Cap bookings per slot to what the bays can actually clear.
- If a slot will slip, say so at booking, not at 09:30 in the waiting room.

## Bottom line

The service itself may be fine once you are on the lift. The booking experience is not: it sells order and delivers a queue. Until the slot means something, treat your Motor X appointment as a rough estimate, not a time to be on time for.

Book the slot if you must, but pad your morning. The clock on the app is decorative.
